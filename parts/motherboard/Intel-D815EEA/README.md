# Intel D815EEA Socket 370 Motherboard

![front](front.jpeg)

## Specifications

- Chipset: Intel 815E (Solano-2)
- CPU socket: Socket 370 (PGA370); supports Intel Pentium III (Coppermine and Coppermine-T) and Celeron (Mendocino and Coppermine-128) processors
- Front-side bus speeds: 66, 100, and 133 MHz
- Form factor: ATX, 304 × 208 mm
- Memory: up to 512MB of SDRAM UDIMMs
- Graphics: integrated Intel i752 graphics with up to 4MB VRAM
- Expansion slots: 5× PCI, 1× universal AGP, and 1× CNR
- I/O: PS/2 keyboard and mouse, floppy, game port, 2× IDE, parallel, 2× serial, 4× USB 1.x, VGA, and 3.5mm audio
- Onboard audio: Analog Devices AD1885 (SoundMAX AC97)
- Onboard LAN: Intel 82562ET 10/100 Ethernet
