## Personal Notes

I put together this system in its current form in 2025 using components acquired from many different places over a long period of time. The oldest of these is the [Baby-AT Desktop Case](Case/README.md) and its power supply, which I bought in 1995. A history of the previous systems this case housed is in the case's [Personal Notes](Case/README.md#personal-notes) section.
